## 🔍 Key findings

1. **Dice performs very well**: 99.1% of samples have Dice ≥ 0.990 and spatial alignment is almost perfect, indicating that the spatial registration of the SyN-fast strategy is very stable.

2. **CC is moderate, with room for improvement**: the mean is only 0.78, and no sample reaches "Excellent" (≥0.85). 74.6% fall in the acceptable interval, and 8.4% (80 samples) need review.

3. **The Dice–CC dissociation is clear**: spatial position is well aligned (Dice≈0.995), but intensity-distribution matching still falls short (CC≈0.78). This means that brain-region positions are matched accurately, but the gray/white-matter intensity values still differ systematically from the MNI template.

4. **Characteristics of the outlier samples**: the lowest-CC samples are mostly **IR-FSPGR / Sag_IR-SPGR** series, together with older MPRAGE (SENSE2, GRAPPA2, etc.); these series differ considerably in contrast from the MNI template.
