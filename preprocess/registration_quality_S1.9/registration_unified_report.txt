V. Key findings
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  1. Dice performs very well
     • 99.1% of samples have Dice ≥ 0.990; the spatial overlap is extremely high
     • mean 0.9947, indicating that rigid/non-rigid alignment is very stable
     • only 9 samples (0.9%) have Dice < 0.990

  2. CC is moderate, with room for improvement
     • mean 0.78; no sample reaches the "Excellent" criterion (≥0.85)
     • 74.6% of samples fall in the "Acceptable" interval [0.75, 0.80)
     • 17.0% of samples reach "Good" (≥0.80)
     • 8.4% of samples (80) have CC < 0.75 and need manual review

  3. The Dice–CC dissociation is clear
     • Dice is very high while CC is moderate, indicating that spatial position is well aligned
     • but intensity-distribution matching still falls short, possibly related to the following factors:
       - the [0.5%, 99.5%] quantile range of histogram matching is too conservative
       - some series (IR-FSPGR) differ considerably in contrast from the MNI template
       - N4 bias-field correction does not fully remove field inhomogeneity

  4. Characteristics of the outlier samples
     • the lowest-CC samples are mostly IR-FSPGR / Sag_IR-SPGR series
     • the highest-MAE samples usually have both low CC and low Dice
     • older series (MPRAGE_Repeat, SENSE2, GRAPPA2) fluctuate more in quality

VI. Recommendations
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  1. For the 80 samples with CC < 0.75:
     • check the raw image quality first (motion artefacts, signal-to-noise ratio)
     • try re-registering with syn_standard (100,70,50) or syn_precise (120,80,60)
     • consider adjusting the histogram-matching parameters to widen the matching range

  2. For IR-FSPGR series:
     • consider building a separate within-group template
     • or adjust the N4 correction parameters and the histogram-matching strategy

  3. Overall assessment:
     • the current registration quality is broadly acceptable for downstream analyses (e.g. segmentation, feature extraction)
     • the very high Dice performance guarantees the accuracy of spatial position
     • the moderate CC level means that intensity-based analyses must be interpreted with care
