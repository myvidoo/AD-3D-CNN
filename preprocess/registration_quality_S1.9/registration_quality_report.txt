  Tukey HSD post-hoc test (CC):
  • MCI vs CN:  p=4.16e-07  (CN significantly better than MCI)

  • CN  vs AD:  p=0.662     (no significant difference)

  → Registration quality in the MCI group is significantly lower than in the CN and AD
    groups, possibly related to moderate atrophy and to larger gray/white-matter contrast
    variation.

VI. Overall assessment and recommendations
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  ✓ Strengths:
    1. The Dice coefficient is very high (0.9947±0.002), indicating very good spatial overlap
    2. 99.1% of samples have Dice≥0.990; rigid alignment is essentially perfect
    3. 91.6% of samples meet the acceptable criterion (CC≥0.75)

  ⚠ Shortcomings:
    1. CC is low overall (mean 0.78); no sample reaches the "Excellent" criterion (≥0.85)
    2. 8.4% of samples (80) have CC<0.75 and need manual review
    3. MCI-group quality is significantly worse than the other two groups, possibly
       related to pathological change

  💡 Recommendations for improvement:
    1. For the 80 samples with CC<0.75, check the raw image quality or try re-registering
       with the syn_standard/syn_precise strategy
    2. For IR-FSPGR series, consider building a separate template or adjusting the
       histogram-matching parameters
    3. For the MCI group, consider a SyN strategy with a higher number of iterations
    4. The Dice–CC dissociation is clear: spatial position is well aligned (high Dice),
       but intensity-distribution matching still has room for improvement (moderate CC)
